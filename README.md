### 1. Bookmark Club – Digital Library & E-Book Reader Flutter UI Kit
![Bookmark Club Overview](./bookmark-club-1.png)
![Bookmark Club Reader & Shelf](./bookmark-club-2.png)

A complete, editorial-style mobile UI template designed for digital libraries, e-book platforms, and book club apps. Includes full **Light Mode**, **Dark Mode**, and **Sepia Reader** themes.

* **Included Screens (6 Views):**
  * **Discover Home (Light & Dark Mode):** Book of the Week hero card, Continue Reading progress tracker, and Trending carousel
  * **Explore & Catalog Search:** Filter chips (`Available Now`, `E-Book`, `Audiobook`), voice search, and ISBN barcode scanner trigger
  * **Book Details:** Format switcher (`E-Book`, `Audiobook`, `Physical`), live availability counter, synopsis, and sample reader trigger
  * **Interactive E-Book Reader:** Drop-cap typography, adjustable font sizing (`A-` / `A+`), chapter progress bar, and `Paper / Sepia / Night` reading modes
  * **My Shelf:** Tabbed library manager (`Reading`, `Loans`, `Saved`, `Finished`) with swipe-to-manage cards
* **Tech Stack:** Flutter 3.x, Dart (Null-Safe, modular widget architecture, iOS & Android responsive)
